# Tamal – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

Tamal is a browser extension that shows Malayalam text in Tamil script.

## What Tamal collects

Nothing. Tamal does not collect, store, send or share any personal information or browsing
data.

## How it works

- **Everything happens in your browser.** Tamal converts text on your own device. It has no
  server, sends no network requests, and runs no analytics or tracking.
- **It only runs when you ask.** Tamal reads a page's text only after you click
  *Convert this page* or choose one of its right-click menu items on that page. It then
  replaces the Malayalam text on that page with Tamil script. Nothing is saved, and the
  original page comes back when you reload it.
- **Text you paste into the popup** is converted on the spot and is gone when the popup closes.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why Tamal needs it |
|---|---|
| `activeTab` | To read and convert the page you are on, only when you click Tamal. |
| `scripting` | To run the converter on that page. |
| `contextMenus` | To add the right-click menu items. |

Tamal does not ask for access to all websites, your history, tabs, cookies or storage.
